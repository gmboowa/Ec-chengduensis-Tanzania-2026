Draft caption for Figure 1
==========================

Figure 1. Recombination-filtered core-genome phylogeny of 43 Enterobacter
chengduensis genomes. The maximum-likelihood tree was inferred with IQ-TREE
version 2.4.0 under the TVM+F+ASC model, selected by ModelFinder under the
Bayesian information criterion, from a Snippy version 4.6.0 core-genome
alignment after predicted recombinant regions were identified and masked with
Gubbins version 3.2.1. Node support is from 1,000 ultrafast bootstrap
replicates; filled nodes indicate support of at least 90%. Isolates sequenced
in this study, A55822 and A55955, are labelled in red. Annotation tracks show,
from the inside outward: country of origin; isolation source as recorded in the
NCBI BioSample record, grouped; the number of acquired antimicrobial resistance
genes detected, banded as low (<=5), medium (6-8) or high (>=9 genes); and the
presence of a plasmid replicon marker and of ybtP/ybtQ. Filled and open symbols
denote presence and absence. Genomes separate into two clusters that correspond
exactly to the two bands of average nucleotide identity against the type-strain
assembly GCF_001984825.2: 22 genomes at 95.67-95.80% and 21 at 99.03-99.99%.
The two clusters differ by 121,375 to 122,118 core-genome SNPs, while distances
within either cluster do not exceed 24,557; the distribution of all pairwise
distances is shown in Supplementary Figure S[n]. Branch lengths differ by
approximately three orders of magnitude between and within clusters, so the
tree is displayed without branch lengths to scale.
